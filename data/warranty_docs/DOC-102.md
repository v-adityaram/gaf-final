# Prototype Wind Warranty Rules

## Standard Limited

Standard nailing pattern. Shingle only. Prototype wind coverage: up to 130 mph.

## WindProven

LayerLock nailing method (special nail zone) plus all four qualifying add-on categories installed as a system:

1. Leak barrier (WeatherWatch or StormGuard)
2. Roof deck protection (Tiger Paw or Deck-Armor)
3. Starter strip (Pro-Start or WeatherBlocker)
4. Ridge cap (Seal-A-Ridge or TimberTex)

When all four categories and the LayerLock method are present the prototype treats WindProven as having no maximum wind-speed limit. If any category is missing, the order does not qualify for the top tier and the assistant must say which category is missing rather than claim eligibility.

| Tier | Wind coverage | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Limited | Up to 130 mph | Standard nailing, shingle only |
| WindProven | No maximum in prototype | LayerLock nailing + leak barrier + roof deck protection + starter strip + ridge cap |
