# Add-On & Ventilation Requirements Guide

Add-on quantity rules used by the Smart Order Helper (prototype):

- Starter strip (Pro-Start): 1 bundle per 10 squares of shingles.
- Roof deck protection (Tiger Paw): 1 roll per 10 squares.
- Leak barrier (WeatherWatch): 1 roll per 20 squares.
- Ridge cap (Seal-A-Ridge, colour-matched): 1 bundle per 3 squares. Only Charcoal and Weathered Wood ridge caps exist in the prototype catalogue; any other shingle colour needs the ridge cap colour confirmed with the customer.
- Ridge ventilation (Cobra Ridge Ventilation): optional, 1 piece per 4 ridge feet.

Timberline UHDZ and Camelot II prefer the premium TimberTex ridge cap where a colour match exists. Ventilation is not a WindProven qualifying category.
